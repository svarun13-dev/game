# Character Classes
## Atlantis: Fall of the Eternal City

---

## Overview

Players choose one of **6 base classes** at the start. Each class offers unique:
- Combat style and abilities
- Resource management (Mana, Solar, Stamina)
- Role in combat (Tank, DPS, Support, Hybrid)
- Narrative flavor and NPC reactions

**Advanced classes** unlock later through story progression and achievements.

---

## Base Classes

### 1. ⚔️ WARRIOR

**Role:** Tank / Melee DPS
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Primary Resource:** Stamina (+ Solar for special moves)

#### Fantasy
The backbone of Atlantean military. Masters of sword, spear, and shield. Unwavering defenders of their city, trained in the ancient martial academies of the Bronze District.

#### Combat Style
- Close-range melee combat
- High defense and health
- Tank enemy hits while dealing consistent damage
- Generates aggro to protect allies (future party system)

#### Starting Weapon
**Bronze Sword & Shield** or **Atlantean Spear**

#### Core Stats Priority
1. **Vitality (VIT)** - Max HP, defense
2. **Strength (STR)** - Physical damage
3. **Dexterity (DEX)** - Attack speed, parry chance

#### Signature Abilities

**1. Shield Bash** (Stamina)
- Melee strike that stuns enemy for 2 seconds
- Cooldown: 8s

**2. Whirlwind Strike** (Stamina)
- Spin attack hitting all nearby enemies
- Cooldown: 12s

**3. Defensive Stance** (Stamina over time)
- Reduce damage taken by 40%, move slower
- Toggle ability

**4. Solar Aegis** (Solar Energy)
- Create golden shield absorbing next 3 hits
- Cooldown: 20s

**5. Titan's Fury** (Ultimate - Stamina + Solar)
- Massive damage boost for 10 seconds
- Unlocks at level 8

#### Skill Tree Branches
- **Guardian Path:** Defense, taunts, protection abilities
- **Berserker Path:** Damage, rage mechanics, heavy hits
- **Phalanx Path:** Balanced, team buffs, counter-attacks

#### Strengths
- High survivability
- Straightforward gameplay
- Strong early game
- Good for beginners

#### Weaknesses
- Limited range
- Lower burst damage than specialists
- Slower movement
- Mana-dependent enemies can kite

### 2. 🔮 MAGE

**Role:** Ranged Magic DPS / Crowd Control
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Primary Resource:** Mana (+ Solar for ultimate)

#### Fantasy
Scholars of the Great Library of Atlantis. Wielders of elemental forces - fire, ice, lightning, and earth. Keepers of ancient magical knowledge predating even Atlantis itself.

#### Combat Style
- Long-range spellcasting
- Area-of-effect damage
- Crowd control (freeze, stun, knockback)
- Glass cannon - high damage, low defense

#### Starting Weapon
**Atlantean Staff** or **Elemental Grimoire**

#### Core Stats Priority
1. **Intelligence (INT)** - Magic damage, Mana pool
2. **Wisdom (WIS)** - Mana regeneration, spell cooldown
3. **Dexterity (DEX)** - Cast speed

#### Signature Abilities

**1. Fireball** (Mana: 15)
- Ranged explosive projectile
- High single-target damage
- Cooldown: 3s

**2. Frost Nova** (Mana: 25)
- AoE freeze around caster
- Freezes enemies for 3 seconds
- Cooldown: 15s

**3. Lightning Bolt** (Mana: 20)
- Instant cast, chain lightning effect
- Bounces to 3 nearby enemies
- Cooldown: 8s

**4. Earth Wall** (Mana: 30)
- Create stone barrier blocking movement/projectiles
- Lasts 8 seconds
- Cooldown: 20s

**5. Solar Cataclysm** (Ultimate - Full Mana + Solar)
- Massive AoE explosion
- Channeled ability
- Unlocks at level 10

#### Skill Tree Branches
- **Pyromancer Path:** Fire damage, burn effects, burst
- **Cryomancer Path:** Ice/water, crowd control, defense
- **Stormcaller Path:** Lightning/wind, mobility, chain effects

#### Strengths
- Highest damage potential
- Excellent AoE clear
- Safe range
- Crowd control utility

#### Weaknesses
- Very fragile (low HP/defense)
- Resource-dependent (no Mana = limited options)
- Vulnerable if enemies close distance
- Requires positioning skill

### 3. 🏹 ARCHER

**Role:** Ranged Physical DPS / Mobility
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Primary Resource:** Focus (special stamina) + Arrows

#### Fantasy
Rangers from the Wildlands who joined Atlantean society. Masters of the bow, trackers, and survivalists. They move like shadows and strike from impossible distances.

#### Combat Style
- Long-range physical attacks
- High mobility (rolls, backflips)
- Kiting and positioning
- Precision over brute force

#### Starting Weapon
**Composite Bow** + **Quiver (∞ basic arrows, special arrows limited)**

#### Core Stats Priority
1. **Dexterity (DEX)** - Damage, attack speed, dodge
2. **Strength (STR)** - Arrow penetration
3. **Luck** - Critical hit chance

#### Signature Abilities

**1. Rapid Shot** (Focus: 10)
- Fire 3 arrows in quick succession
- Cooldown: 5s

**2. Explosive Arrow** (Special Arrow x1)
- AoE explosion on impact
- Cooldown: 12s

**3. Eagle Eye** (Passive)
- Zoom vision, highlight weak points
- Critical hits deal 150% damage

**4. Evasive Roll** (Focus: 5)
- Quick dodge with i-frames
- Can be chained up to 3 times
- Cooldown: 2s between rolls

**5. Solar Volley** (Ultimate - Solar Energy)
- Rain of golden arrows from sky
- Covers large area
- Unlocks at level 9

#### Skill Tree Branches
- **Marksman Path:** Critical hits, precision, single-target
- **Trapper Path:** Trick arrows, traps, crowd control
- **Windrunner Path:** Mobility, speed buffs, evasion

#### Strengths
- Excellent mobility
- Safe distance combat
- High critical damage
- Fun, skill-based gameplay

#### Weaknesses
- Squishy (low defense)
- Ammo management for special arrows
- Struggles against swarms
- Requires good aim and positioning

### 4. ✨ PRIEST

**Role:** Healer / Support / Holy DPS
**Difficulty:** Medium-Hard
**Primary Resource:** Divine Mana (+ Solar for buffs)

#### Fantasy
Devoted servants of the Atlantean Pantheon. Channelers of divine light and holy magic. They heal the wounded, bless the faithful, and smite corruption with righteous fury.

#### Combat Style
- Balanced offense and support
- Healing abilities
- Holy damage (effective vs undead/dark creatures)
- Buff/debuff specialist

#### Starting Weapon
**Sacred Mace** or **Blessed Talisman**

#### Core Stats Priority
1. **Wisdom (WIS)** - Healing power, Mana regen, buff strength
2. **Intelligence (INT)** - Mana pool, holy damage
3. **Vitality (VIT)** - Survivability

#### Signature Abilities

**1. Healing Light** (Divine Mana: 20)
- Restore 40% HP to self
- (Later: can target allies)
- Cooldown: 15s

**2. Smite** (Divine Mana: 15)
- Holy damage projectile
- Extra damage vs undead/demons
- Cooldown: 4s

**3. Holy Shield** (Divine Mana: 25)
- Barrier preventing next instance of damage
- Lasts 10s or until broken
- Cooldown: 20s

**4. Purify** (Divine Mana: 30)
- Remove debuffs (poison, curse, slow)
- Small AoE heal
- Cooldown: 18s

**5. Divine Ascension** (Ultimate - Solar Energy)
- Wings of light, flight for 8s
- Massive healing AoE pulse
- Immunity to damage during flight
- Unlocks at level 10

#### Skill Tree Branches
- **Oracle Path:** Healing, buffs, defense
- **Templar Path:** Holy damage, offensive, judgement
- **Zealot Path:** Hybrid, self-buffs, combat priest

#### Strengths
- Self-sustain through healing
- Versatile (damage + support)
- Great for solo play
- Essential for future party content

#### Weaknesses
- Jack-of-all-trades (not best at anything)
- Resource management crucial
- Lower burst damage than pure DPS
- Requires tactical thinking

### 5. 🗡️ ROGUE

**Role:** Assassin / Burst DPS / Stealth
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Primary Resource:** Energy (fast regen) + Combo Points

#### Fantasy
Shadow dwellers of Atlantis's underbelly. Thieves, assassins, spies. Masters of stealth, poison, and the killing blow. They strike from darkness and vanish before retaliation.

#### Combat Style
- Melee burst damage
- Stealth mechanics
- Combo point system (build → spend)
- High risk, high reward
- Backstab bonus damage

#### Starting Weapon
**Twin Daggers** or **Poisoned Blade**

#### Core Stats Priority
1. **Dexterity (DEX)** - Damage, attack speed, dodge
2. **Luck** - Critical hit chance & damage
3. **Strength (STR)** - Base damage

#### Signature Abilities

**1. Backstab** (Energy: 20)
- Massive damage from behind
- Builds 2 Combo Points
- Cooldown: 6s

**2. Shadowstep** (Energy: 30)
- Teleport behind target
- Next attack guaranteed crit
- Cooldown: 12s

**3. Stealth** (Energy: 40, drains over time)
- Become invisible
- Breaks on attack or taking damage
- Movement speed reduced
- Cooldown: 25s

**4. Eviscerate** (Combo Points: 5)
- Finisher move
- Damage scales with Combo Points spent
- Cooldown: 1s

**5. Shadow Dance** (Ultimate - Solar Energy)
- Unlimited Energy for 10s
- All abilities build +1 Combo Point
- Unlocks at level 9

#### Skill Tree Branches
- **Assassin Path:** Burst damage, stealth, single-target
- **Duelist Path:** Sustained DPS, parrying, sword combat
- **Poisoner Path:** DoTs, debuffs, crowd control

#### Combo Point System
- Build points with specific abilities (max 5)
- Spend on powerful finishers
- Adds skill ceiling and planning

#### Strengths
- Highest burst damage in game
- Stealth allows creative approaches
- Extremely fun for skilled players
- Critical hit fantasy

#### Weaknesses
- Squishiest class
- Very punishing if caught
- Complex resource management
- Hard to master

### 6. 🐺 ANIMALIST

**Role:** Summoner / Shapeshifter / Nature Magic
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Primary Resource:** Mana + Beast Bond

#### Fantasy
Children of the Wildlands, blessed by ancient nature spirits. They commune with beasts, shift into animal forms, and channel primal fury. Atlantis fears and respects their savage power.

#### Combat Style
- **Two modes:** Human form (caster) / Beast form (melee)
- Summon animal companions
- Nature magic (thorns, vines, poison)
- Form-shifting mid-combat

#### Starting Weapon
**Druidic Staff** or **Bone Totem**

#### Core Stats Priority
1. **Wisdom (WIS)** - Pet damage, Mana, nature magic
2. **Strength (STR)** - Beast form damage
3. **Vitality (VIT)** - Survivability in beast form

#### Signature Abilities (Human Form)

**1. Summon Wolf** (Mana: 40, Beast Bond: 1)
- Call wolf companion to fight
- Lasts until killed or dismissed
- Max 1 active companion (more later)
- No cooldown (but requires resources)

**2. Entangling Roots** (Mana: 25)
- Root enemies in place for 4s
- AoE ground target
- Cooldown: 14s

**3. Thorn Volley** (Mana: 15)
- Ranged nature damage projectile
- Applies poison DoT
- Cooldown: 5s

#### Signature Abilities (Beast Form)

**4. Transform: Bear** (Mana: 50, toggle)
- Shift into bear form
- +60% HP, +40% armor
- Melee attacks, different abilities
- Can't cast spells in beast form

**5. Savage Roar** (Beast Bond, in bear form)
- AoE fear, enemies flee for 3s
- Buff self: +30% damage
- Cooldown: 20s

**6. Primal Rage** (Ultimate - Solar Energy)
- Transform into legendary beast (later: choice of forms)
- Massive stat boost
- Lasts 15s
- Unlocks at level 10

#### Beast Bond System
- Resource that builds from dealing damage
- Spent on empowering summons or transformations
- Adds unique resource juggling

#### Skill Tree Branches
- **Beastmaster Path:** Pet focus, summon variety, pet buffs
- **Shapeshifter Path:** Transform forms, hybrid combat
- **Naturalist Path:** Nature magic, DoTs, healing

#### Strengths
- Versatile (ranged magic + melee forms)
- Pet can tank damage
- Unique playstyle
- High skill ceiling = high satisfaction

#### Weaknesses
- Complexity (manage pet, forms, resources)
- Pet AI can be unpredictable
- Weaker if pet dies
- Split stat needs (INT for spells, STR for forms)

## Recommended Starting Class

**For Demo/Prototype Development:**

**Mage** is recommended to build first because:
1. Ranged combat easier to implement
2. Shows off visual effects (fireballs, ice, lightning)
3. Resource management (Mana) is core mechanic
4. Less complex than Rogue/Animalist
5. Can showcase both magic systems

**Alternative:** Warrior (simplest, good for testing core combat)

---

## Design Notes

- Each class should feel **distinctly different** to play
- Encourage **experimentation** through low respec cost (demo: free)
- **Balance philosophy:** Fun > perfect balance (single-player focus)
- **Accessibility:** Easy classes for newcomers, hard classes for veterans
- **Future party system:** Classes complement each other
