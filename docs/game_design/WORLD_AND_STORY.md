# World Design & Story Outline
## Atlantis: Fall of the Eternal City

---

## 🌍 World Overview

### Setting: The Atlantean Empire (Pre-Flood)

**Time Period:** Mythological era, ~9600 BCE (Plato's dating)
**Technological Level:** Bronze Age + Atlantean super-science
**Magic Level:** High - gods are real, magic flows freely
**Tone:** Epic grandeur tinged with doom

### The Island of Atlantis

Inspired by Plato's descriptions with JRPG flair:

**Geography:**
- Massive island in the Atlantic Ocean
- Concentric rings of land and water
- Central island houses the capital city
- Surrounded by mountains protecting fertile plains
- Mediterranean/Greek climate

**Size:**
- Full map (future): ~15-20 distinct zones
- Demo map: Central Atlantis + Western outskirts (~3-4 areas)

## 🏛️ DEMO WORLD MAP

### Playable Areas for Demo

#### 1. CENTRAL ATLANTIS - The Golden District

**Description:**
The heart of the empire. Gleaming marble buildings with gold-trimmed columns, wide plazas with fountains, Solar Crystal obelisks powering the city's wonders.

**Key Locations:**

**The Grand Plaza**
- Central hub area
- Quest givers, merchants
- Solar Obelisk (interactive landmark)
- Fountain of Wisdom (Mana well)

**Temple of Poseidon**
- Massive stepped pyramid structure
- Priests worshipping the sea god
- Early quest location
- Tutorial for Priest class mechanics (if chosen)

**The Academy**
- School of magic and science
- Mage trainers
- Library with lore tablets
- Quest: "Retrieve stolen scroll"

**Royal Barracks**
- Warrior training grounds
- Sparring arena (optional mini-challenges)
- Armory/equipment vendor

**Market Quarter**
- Shops: weapons, armor, potions, general goods
- NPCs with minor quests
- Lively atmosphere, ambient dialogue

**Harbor View**
- Overlook of the ocean
- Fishermen NPCs
- Quest start: "Something in the waters"

**Atmosphere:**
- Bustling, golden hour lighting
- NPCs going about daily life
- Music: Grand, hopeful orchestral
- No combat (safe zone)

#### 2. WESTERN QUARTER - Residential District

**Description:**
Where common citizens live. Smaller buildings, more modest. Families, children, artisans. Shows the "real" Atlantis beyond the glamour.

**Key Locations:**

**Hero's Home**
- Starting location (if we give hero a backstory)
- Small house with family NPC(s)
- Can rest here to restore HP/Mana/Solar
- Personal storage chest

**Artisan Row**
- Blacksmith, fletcher, tailor
- Upgrade equipment
- Meet craftsmen characters

**The Drunken Dolphin (Tavern)**
- Social hub
- Rumors and gossip
- Side quests picked up here
- Gambling mini-game? (optional)

**City Gates (West)**
- Transition to outdoor zone
- Guards warn of dangers outside
- Quest: "Clear the road of beasts"

**Atmosphere:**
- Warm, lived-in feel
- Music: Gentle, homely
- Minimal combat (maybe rats in cellar quest)

#### 3. THE OUTER GARDENS - Farmland & Wilderness Transition

**Description:**
Agricultural zone outside city walls. Fields, vineyards, small farming communities. Gradually becomes wilder toward the edges.

**Key Locations:**

**Farmsteads**
- Friendly NPCs (farmers, shepherds)
- Quest: "Wolves killed my sheep"
- Tutorial area for combat

**Olive Grove**
- Peaceful area with some resources
- Minor enemies: Wild boars, aggressive birds

**Abandoned Shrine**
- Small ruined temple
- Light puzzle (move blocks to reveal door?)
- Treasure chest
- Lore tablet about "old gods"

**Forest Edge**
- Denser trees
- Stronger enemies: Wolves, giant spiders
- Leads to Coastal Cliffs

**Atmosphere:**
- Pastoral, then gradually ominous
- Music: Starts peaceful, becomes tense
- First real combat challenges

#### 4. COASTAL CLIFFS - First Dungeon

**Description:**
Rocky coastline with cave systems. The "first dungeon" of the demo. Rumors of strange creatures emerging from the deep.

**Key Locations:**

**The Tide Pools**
- Exterior area with water hazards
- Enemies: Crabs, sea serpents
- Environmental puzzle: Use tides (timing-based)

**Smuggler's Cave (Main Dungeon)**
- 3-4 interconnected rooms
- Light, switch, and combat puzzles
- Enemy variety: Bandits, cave bats, possessed statues
- Mini-boss: Corrupted Sea Nymph or Bandit Leader

**Hidden Grotto**
- Secret area (requires exploration)
- Rare loot
- Lore: Ancient pictographs hinting at flood prophecy

**The Abyss Gate**
- Locked door with ominous symbols
- Can't open in demo
- "This way is sealed... for now"
- Hint at future content

**Atmosphere:**
- Dark, mysterious, dangerous
- Music: Tense exploration, intense combat
- Proper dungeon crawling experience

## 📜 STORY OUTLINE

### Premise

You are a young citizen of Atlantis in its golden age. The city is at the height of its power, wielding Solar Energy technology that rivals the gods themselves. But cracks are showing: strange omens, creatures emerging from the deep, earthquakes shaking the foundations.

A prophecy speaks of a Great Flood that will swallow the empire. Most dismiss it as superstition. But you will discover the terrible truth... and face impossible choices.

### Main Story Arc (Full Game - 3 Acts)

---

#### ACT 1: THE OMEN

**Setup:**
- Hero lives ordinary life in Atlantis
- Choose your path (class selection) based on background
- Strange events begin: earthquakes, animals fleeing, red tides

**Inciting Incident:**
- Vision/dream of drowning city (all players experience this)
- Seek answers at Temple of Poseidon
- Priest reveals ancient prophecy

**Act 1 Quests:**
- Investigate mysterious attacks on farms (Outer Gardens)
- Explore Smuggler's Cave, discover corrupted creatures
- Learn about Solar Energy's dark side
- First moral choice: Save scientist or destroy dangerous research

**Act 1 Climax:**
- Confront corrupted guardian creature (boss)
- Prophecy confirmed: Flood is coming
- Timeline: "When the stars align..." (player has time, but countdown begins)

**Act 1 End:**
- Hero resolves to find way to stop/survive flood
- Factions emerge: Scientists think Solar can save them, Priests say it's the cause

---

#### ACT 2: THE SEARCH

**Rising Action:**
- Travel to other cities seeking answers
- Recruit allies (NPCs, future companions?)
- Uncover conspiracy: Some know about flood, hiding truth

**Major Quests:**
- Azoria: Ancient tablet speaks of "those who came before" (previous civilization)
- Poseidonis: Naval expedition to underwater ruins
- Wildlands: Commune with nature spirits who remember last cataclysm

**Character Development:**
- Flashbacks to Atlantis's founding
- Moral choices about using Solar Energy
- Romance subplot? (optional)

**Mid-Act Crisis:**
- Solar Crystal Array malfunctions
- Massive earthquake
- Casualties, destruction
- Timer becomes visible/urgent

**Act 2 Climax:**
- Discover truth in Deep Catacombs
- Solar Energy IS destabilizing the world
- But shutting it down will leave Atlantis defenseless against flood
- Impossible choice setup

---

#### ACT 3: THE FLOOD

**The Dilemma:**
- **Choice A:** Destroy Solar Arrays, appease gods, maybe prevent flood (sacrifice progress)
- **Choice B:** Enhance Solar Arrays, create shield, save Atlantis (defy gods)
- **Choice C:** Middle path - partial shutdown, selective salvation (save some, not all)

**Consequences:**
- NPCs react based on your previous choices
- Factions support or oppose you
- Some characters may die/be saved based on decisions

**Final Quests:**
- Rally your chosen faction
- Final dungeon: Heart of Solar Array OR Temple of the Gods
- Boss fight: Corrupted Atlantean Guardian OR Divine Avatar (depends on path)

**The Flood Event:**
- Epic cutscene/playable sequence
- Waters rise, destruction
- Outcome varies by player choices

**Endings:**

**Ending 1: Divine Mercy**
- Destroyed Solar Arrays
- Flood comes but less severe
- Atlantis survives, humbled
- Return to simpler ways

**Ending 2: Mortal Triumph**
- Enhanced Solar Shield
- Flood repelled temporarily
- Atlantis stands but at great cost
- Ominous future (gods will return)

**Ending 3: Sacrifice**
- Middle path chosen
- Atlantis sinks but people escape
- Hero ensures survivors reach mainland
- Bittersweet - culture preserved, city lost

**Ending 4: Hubris (Bad End)**
- Made all wrong choices
- Total destruction
- Hero survives alone on driftwood
- Somber, tragic

### DEMO STORY (Condensed - Act 1 Beginning)

**Length:** 1-2 hours

**Story Beats:**

1. **Opening:**
   - Brief text crawl establishing Atlantis
   - Hero wakes up from prophetic nightmare (flood vision)
   - Choose class (framed as "what is your path?")

2. **Tutorial:**
   - Learn controls in safe environment (Western Quarter)
   - Talk to family/friend NPC who explains basics
   - Visit Market Quarter (shop tutorial)

3. **First Quest:**
   - Elder at Grand Plaza: "Farms report attacks, investigate"
   - Travel to Outer Gardens
   - Combat tutorial against wolves/boars

4. **Investigation:**
   - Talk to scared farmers
   - Clues point to Coastal Cliffs
   - Optional side quests in area

5. **Dungeon:**
   - Enter Smuggler's Cave
   - Light puzzles and combat
   - Discover corrupted creatures (unnatural, Solar-touched?)

6. **Mini-Boss:**
   - Corrupted Sea Nymph or Bandit Leader fused with crystal
   - First major combat challenge
   - Drops rare loot

7. **Revelation:**
   - Find smuggler's journal/notes
   - Hints at Solar Crystal experiments going wrong
   - "Something is wrong in the deep..."

8. **Demo End:**
   - Return to Atlantis
   - Report findings to Council or Priest
   - Ominous dialogue: "This is only the beginning..."
   - **TO BE CONTINUED** screen
   - Show statue/concept art of full Atlantis with flood waters rising (tease)

## 🎨 Environmental Storytelling

Scattered throughout world, readable for lore:

**Topics:**
- Founding of Atlantis
- Discovery of Solar Crystals
- Wars with other nations
- Mythology of the Pantheon
- Previous civilizations (Lemuria?)
- Prophecies and omens

**Demo Tablets (5-7 total):**
1. "The Gift of Poseidon" - Atlantis's founding
2. "Solar Dawn" - First Solar Crystal activation
3. "The Concentric Rings" - City's construction
4. "Beasts of the Deep" - Mythological creatures
5. "Whispers of Doom" - First prophecy mention

### Visual Storytelling

**Show, don't tell:**
- Cracks in marble buildings (subtle, foreshadowing)
- Solar Crystals flickering occasionally
- NPCs praying more frequently
- Animals acting strangely
- Water levels slightly higher each visit (if time progression)

## ⚖️ MORAL CHOICE SYSTEM

### Philosophy

- **No clear good/evil** - Shades of gray
- **Consequences, not karma** - Choices matter narratively, not stat-wise
- **Factions, not alignment** - Who do you support?

### Demo Choices (Minor)

**Choice 1: The Theft**
- Catch thief stealing bread
- **Option A:** Turn them in (law and order)
- **Option B:** Let them go (compassion)
- **Option C:** Pay for bread yourself (sacrifice)
- *Effect:* Changes NPC dialogue, minor reputation shift

**Choice 2: The Experiment**
- Scientist's Solar experiment is dangerous
- **Option A:** Help complete it (knowledge)
- **Option B:** Sabotage it (caution)
- *Effect:* Scientist becomes ally/enemy, affects mini-boss difficulty

### Full Game Choices (Major)

- Save City A or City B from attack
- Kill or spare antagonist
- Align with Science faction or Tradition faction
- Sacrifice yourself or let another die
- Final decision about Solar Arrays

## 🎯 Design Goals

### World Design

- **Believable:** Atlantis feels like a real place
- **Explorable:** Reward curiosity
- **Layered:** Surface beauty, underlying dread
- **Memorable:** Iconic landmarks and moments

### Narrative

- **Player-driven:** Choices feel meaningful
- **Epic scope:** Gods, prophecies, civilization's fall
- **Personal stakes:** Not just world, but people you care about
- **Satisfying arc:** Setup → conflict → resolution

### Tone Balance

- **70% Epic Adventure** - Fun, exciting, wonder
- **20% Mystery** - Uncovering secrets
- **10% Dread** - Looming doom, tension
