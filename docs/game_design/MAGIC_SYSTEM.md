# Magic System
## Atlantis: Fall of the Eternal City

---

## Core Concept

Two distinct energy systems power abilities in this world:

1. **Mana** - Ancient, natural magic force
2. **Solar Energy** - Atlantean technological advancement

This duality represents the game's core theme: **tradition vs progress**, **nature vs technology**.

---

## 🌊 MANA (Traditional Magic)

### Description
The primordial energy that flows through all living things. Used since time immemorial by priests, druids, and wizards. Mana is the breath of the gods, the pulse of the earth.

### Visual Identity
- **Color:** Deep blue with silver sparkles
- **Particle Effects:** Flowing water-like streams, starlight motes
- **Sound:** Ethereal chimes, resonant hums

### Mechanics

**Regeneration:**
- **Passive:** 2% max Mana per second (out of combat)
- **Combat:** 1% max Mana per second
- **Rest/Meditation:** 10% per second (channeled action)
- **Potions:** Instant restoration
- **Level up:** Full restore

**Maximum Pool:**
- Base: 100 Mana
- +10 per level
- +5 per point in Intelligence
- Scales with equipment

**Consumption:**
- Abilities cost 10-50 Mana per cast
- More powerful spells = higher cost
- No "out of Mana" penalty (just can't cast)

### Who Uses It?
**Primary Users:**
- Mage (heavy usage)
- Priest (Divine Mana variant)
- Animalist (moderate usage)

**Secondary Users:**
- Warrior (rare, for specific abilities)
- Archer (some trick arrows)
- Rogue (almost never)

### Associated Powers
- Elemental magic (fire, ice, lightning, earth)
- Healing and restoration
- Nature magic (vines, animals, growth)
- Divination and protective wards
- Curses and debuffs

### Lore
*"Mana is the gift of the Pantheon, the essence that connects mortal to divine. To wield Mana is to walk in the footsteps of gods."*

## ☀️ SOLAR ENERGY (Atlantean Power)

### Description
The revolutionary discovery of Atlantean science - crystallized sunlight, divine energy made tangible. Solar Energy is what elevated Atlantis above all civilizations, powering their golden age.

### Visual Identity
- **Color:** Radiant gold, amber, brilliant white
- **Particle Effects:** Sun rays, golden sparks, light blooms
- **Sound:** Powerful bass hums, crystalline chimes, energy crackles

### Mechanics

**Generation (Charge System):**
- **Combat Actions:** Gain 5-15 Solar per hit dealt
- **Sunlight Exposure:** +1 Solar per second (outdoor, daytime)
- **Taking Damage:** +3 Solar per hit received
- **Solar Crystals:** Special pickups that grant 50 Solar
- **Executing enemies:** +25 Solar bonus

**Maximum Pool:**
- Base: 100 Solar Energy
- +8 per level
- +3 per point in Wisdom
- Enhanced by special Atlantean equipment

**Consumption:**
- Ultimate abilities: 80-100 Solar
- Enhanced abilities: 30-50 Solar
- Solar-infused attacks: 15-25 Solar
- Powerful but limited use

**Day/Night Cycle Effects:**
- **Daytime (Outdoor):**
  - +50% generation rate
  - Abilities cost 10% less
  - Visual effects more brilliant

- **Nighttime (Outdoor):**
  - Normal generation
  - Standard costs

- **Indoors/Underground:**
  - -25% generation rate
  - Abilities cost 10% more
  - Strategic consideration for dungeon crawling

### Who Uses It?
**ALL classes use Solar Energy**, but differently:

- **Warrior:** Defensive shields, powerful strikes
- **Mage:** Massive AoE ultimate spells
- **Archer:** Arrow barrages from the sky
- **Priest:** Divine transformation, mass healing
- **Rogue:** Shadow techniques, burst combos
- **Animalist:** Primal beast transformations

### Associated Powers
- Atlantean technology activation
- Enhanced physical abilities
- Divine manifestations
- Massive AoE attacks
- Transformation abilities
- Defensive barriers of light

### Lore
*"We have captured the sun itself. The gods gave mankind fire; we have given ourselves stars."*
— High Priest Azarion, on the invention of Solar Crystals

### The Dark Secret
Solar Energy is harvested through massive crystal arrays that may be destabilizing the very fabric of reality. Some whisper this hubris is why the gods will send the flood...

## ⚖️ Hybrid Abilities

Some advanced abilities require **BOTH resources** simultaneously, representing the fusion of ancient magic and modern technology.

### Examples

**Solar Infusion** (Mage)
- Cost: 30 Mana + 40 Solar
- Effect: Next spell deals double damage and pierces all resistances
- Cooldown: 25s

**Divine Sunburst** (Priest)
- Cost: 40 Divine Mana + 60 Solar
- Effect: Massive AoE heal + damage to undead
- Cooldown: 40s

**Celestial Arrow** (Archer)
- Cost: 25 Focus + 45 Solar
- Effect: Arrow that splits into 10 seeking projectiles
- Cooldown: 30s

### Design Philosophy
- Hybrid abilities are **rare and powerful**
- Reward resource management mastery
- Feel impactful and special
- Represent character growth and enlightenment

## 🔮 Resource Management Strategies

### For Mana-Heavy Classes (Mage, Priest)

**Conservative Play:**
- Use basic attacks between spells
- Save Mana for important moments
- Carry Mana potions

**Aggressive Play:**
- Spend freely, rely on regeneration
- High INT stat for bigger pool
- Risk running dry for maximum DPS

### For Solar-Dependent Classes (All)

**Farming Solar:**
- Fight more enemies to charge up
- Stay outdoors when possible
- Seek Solar Crystal nodes in world

**Ultimate Timing:**
- Save for boss fights
- Use on tough elite enemies
- Emergency panic button

**Offensive Style:**
- Deal damage = generate Solar
- Aggressive play = more resources
- Rewards skill and combat engagement

### Hybrid Resource Users (Animalist)

**Balancing Act:**
- Mana for summons/spells (human form)
- Solar for beast transformations
- Beast Bond as third resource layer
- Most complex but most versatile

## 🌍 Environmental Interactions

### Solar Energy World Mechanics

**Sunlight:**
- Open plains: Maximum generation
- Forest canopy: Reduced generation
- Caves/dungeons: Minimal generation
- Night cycles: Standard generation

**Solar Crystals (World Objects):**
- Found scattered in world
- Glowing golden crystals
- Interact to gain +50 Solar instantly
- Respawn after 5 minutes
- Some hidden, some in dangerous locations

**Solar Obelisks (Landmarks):**
- Ancient Atlantean structures
- Stand near for +5 Solar/second
- Often guarded or in puzzle areas
- Fast-travel points later in game

### Mana Wells (World Objects)

**Mana Springs:**
- Natural magical sites
- Blue glowing pools
- Stand in to regenerate 5 Mana/second
- Sometimes quests involve protecting them

**Ley Lines:**
- Invisible magic currents
- Discovered with Priest's "Detect Magic" ability
- Following them leads to treasures
- Boost Mana regen by 100% while near

## 🎲 Advanced Mechanics

### Resource Conversion (Late Game Ability?)

**Solar to Mana:**
- Convert 50 Solar → 40 Mana
- For emergency spellcasting
- Cooldown: 60s

**Mana to Solar:**
- Convert 50 Mana → 30 Solar
- Less efficient (technology < magic)
- Cooldown: 60s

**Unlock:** Special quest or skill tree talent

### Over-Channeling (Risk/Reward)

**Concept:** Spend resources you don't have for bonus power

**Mana Over-Channel:**
- Cast spell even if lacking Mana
- Takes from HP instead (1:1 ratio)
- Spell gets +20% power
- Dangerous but clutch

**Solar Overcharge:**
- Spend more Solar than ability needs
- Each +10 Solar spent = +15% damage
- Max: +100 Solar for double damage
- High skill ceiling mechanic

## ⚔️ Combat Flow Example

**Mage vs Group of Enemies:**

1. **Opening** (Full Mana, Low Solar)
   - Fireball (cost: 15 Mana) → Hit 3 enemies
   - Gain: +30 Solar from damage dealt

2. **Mid Fight** (Depleting Mana, Building Solar)
   - Frost Nova (cost: 25 Mana) → Freeze enemies
   - Lightning Chain (cost: 20 Mana) → More damage
   - Gain: +45 Solar total now

3. **Ultimate** (Low Mana, Full Solar)
   - Solar Cataclysm (cost: 100 Solar + 30 Mana)
   - Massive explosion finishes fight
   - Victory!

4. **After Combat**
   - Mana regenerates passively
   - Solar decays slowly or persists to next fight (design choice)

## 🧪 Balancing Considerations

### Design Goals

1. **Both systems feel valuable** - No "dump stat" resource
2. **Different playstyles supported** - Mana-heavy vs Solar-heavy builds
3. **Interplay creates depth** - Managing both is more interesting than one
4. **Clear visual feedback** - Always know resource states
5. **Refill pacing** - Never waiting too long to play

### Tuning Levers

- Regeneration rates
- Ability costs
- Solar generation from hits
- Day/night multipliers
- Potion availability and effectiveness
- Level scaling of max pools

### Testing Checklist

- [ ] Can player cast ~5 Mana spells before running dry?
- [ ] Does Solar generate fast enough in combat?
- [ ] Are ultimates available ~2-3 times per boss fight?
- [ ] Does day/night cycle feel impactful but not punishing?
- [ ] Do hybrid abilities feel worth the dual cost?
