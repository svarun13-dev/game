## 🌊 About

Experience the final days of the greatest civilization ever known. Play as a young hero navigating Atlantis at its peak - a gleaming city of marble and gold, powered by revolutionary Solar Energy technology. But dark omens threaten paradise: strange creatures emerge from the deep, earthquakes shake the foundations, and ancient prophecies speak of a coming deluge.

**Will you save Atlantis, or witness its inevitable fall?**

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎮 Gameplay
- **Action-Based Combat** - Real-time, skill-driven battles
- **6 Playable Classes** - Warrior, Mage, Archer, Priest, Rogue, Animalist
- **Open World Exploration** - Discover Atlantis and surrounding lands
- **Dual Magic System** - Traditional Mana + Atlantean Solar Energy
- **Moral Choices** - Shape the story and determine the fate of civilization

### 🌍 World
- **Rich Lore** - Greek mythology meets lost civilization mystery
- **Dynamic Environment** - Day/night cycle affects Solar magic
- **Mythological Creatures** - Face legends brought to life
- **Multiple Endings** - Your choices determine Atlantis's fate

### 🎨 Style
- **JRPG Aesthetics** - 2D sprite-based graphics, detailed animations
- **Epic Atmosphere** - Orchestral music with Mediterranean flair
- **Cinematic Storytelling** - Cutscenes, dialogue, character moments

## 🌟 Unique Mechanics

### Dual Magic System

**Mana (Traditional Magic)**
- Passive regeneration
- Used for spells and abilities
- Blue, ethereal aesthetic

**Solar Energy (Atlantean Technology)**
- Charges from combat and sunlight
- Powers ultimate abilities
- Gold, radiant aesthetic
- Affected by day/night cycle

This creates strategic depth: manage both resources, time your ultimates, and choose when to fight based on time of day.

## 📖 Story

### Setting
The mythological island of Atlantis, ~9600 BCE. An advanced civilization at its peak, wielding technology that rivals the gods. But hubris has consequences...

### Premise
You are a young hero who experiences a prophetic vision of Atlantis drowning beneath the waves. As strange omens multiply and creatures emerge from the depths, you must uncover the truth about the coming Great Flood.

**Will you prevent the catastrophe, or ensure some survive when the waters rise?**

### Themes
- Hubris vs humility
- Progress vs tradition
- Fate vs free will
- Sacrifice for the greater good
